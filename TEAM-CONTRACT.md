## How we decide
Small things: whoever is doing the work decides. Big things (scope, stack, pivots): majority vote (at least 2 of 3 members).

## When we work
Coordination: Messenger group chat, asynchronously. No fixed weekly meetings. Members communicate task progress and deadlines in the group.

## Contribution
Work is visible in commits and the journal. If someone carries or someone coasts, we say it in week terms, not in Week 15.

## When it breaks
Step 1: name it inside the team within 48 hours. Step 2: if unresolved in one week, we bring it to the instructor together. If someone misses agreed work or is unresponsive, contact them through Messenger first. If unresolved within one week, involve the instructor.
